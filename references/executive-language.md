# Executive Language Quick Reference

Maps raw output-language phrases to impact-language equivalents for use in Connect review drafting.

Replace these examples with phrases from your own work logs. The pattern is what matters: raw output language on the left, impact language on the right.

The right side adds framing only: who it helped and why it mattered. It never adds a number, a result or a detail that isn't in your log. Where a number would help and your log doesn't have one, the draft leaves a `[number from your log]` placeholder and the gap analysis asks you for it.

| From (raw output language) | To (impact language) |
|---|---|
| "Created training video so new users can onboard themselves" | "Built a reusable training video so new users could onboard themselves" |
| "Answered questions about the new process from three teams" | "Cleared up the new process for three teams so their rollout could proceed" |
| "Shared the tool with another team, they run it on their own now" | "Extended the tool to a second team, which now runs it without our help" |
| "Wrote a handoff doc" | "Turned a verbal handoff into a documented process the next owner can follow" |
| "Ran two project cycles at once" | "Ran two project cycles in parallel" |
| "Finished the data migration, [number from your log] records" | "Completed the data migration of [number from your log] records" |
